Mandatory and Statutory Training

MAST ensures all staff have the essential knowledge and skills to work safely, effectively, and in line with legal and organisational requirements.

This page provides guidance on enrolling in, completing, and monitoring your required training — including links to learning resources, frequently asked questions, and support if you need assistance.

MAST Guide

Expand each section below for step-by-step instructions on identifying, booking, and completing your training.

You can identify your outstanding training modules through the Electronic Staff Record using the following steps:

  1. Navigate to the My ESR Dashboard.
  2. Go to the ‘My Compliance’ Portlet. Your compliance is represented by a red/green bar. If you have a red section, there is training you need to do.
  3. Click on the down arrow next to the bar to see the courses you need to do.
  4. Click on the radio button next to the course you want to do.
